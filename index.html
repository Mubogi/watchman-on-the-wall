    Three questions to sit with

      How to keep the wall

      You are the watchman of a small neighborhood congregation.

      The rhythm

      Members walk the village. Click any of them to look closer. Hearts drift quietly; burdens are hidden until you look — or until your alertness is high enough to see the silent strugglers glowing faintly at the edges of town.

      Keep Watch spends prayer oil to raise alertness. Alertness decays, so the wall must be kept. High alertness reveals approaching threats earlier — giving you time to sound a warning before they reach the gate.

      Burdens grow heavier over time. If a burden reaches its full weight, that member falls away. Bring it back to zero and they are restored.

      The four care actions

      • Secret Intercession — costs prayer oil. Softens a heart from afar, and makes later truth-telling safe. Best for shame, pride and fear.
      • Home Visit & Meal — costs food and time. Heals isolation and practical need. Best for grief, financial strain, family tension.
      • Gentle Restoration & Truth — costs counsel and time. Rescues from deception without pride. Unsafe on a hard heart — intercede first.
      • Pair with a Mature Mentor — costs time and counsel. Builds lasting discipleship. Best for hunger for the Word, weariness, and new believers.

      Galatians 6:1 — restore gently. A harsh word to a hard heart wounds twice.
